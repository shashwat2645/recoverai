Why Business Policies (RAG) in RecoverAI?

In fintech, autonomous AI agents cannot be unpredictable black boxes. RecoverAI transforms your company operating guidelines into semantic vector embeddings using Google Gemini text-embedding-004. When a transaction fails, the agent vector-searches your indexed policies via cosine similarity and grounds its root-cause diagnosis and recovery actions strictly within your rules.
